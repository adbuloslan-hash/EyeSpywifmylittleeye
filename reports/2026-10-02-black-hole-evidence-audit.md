# EyeSpy — Black-Hole Evidence Audit
**Adversarial source/claim audit — 2026-10-02**

## Mandate
Stress-test the EyeSpy thesis rather than assuming it is true. Every proposition is separated into observable mechanism, evidence quality, alternatives, missing evidence, and falsification tests.

## Audit matrix
### Commercial surveillance
**Evidence:** High. FTC documentation directly supports broad collection, retention, sharing, profiling, and monetization.
**Next audit:** company-specific retention periods, broker relationships, SDKs, ad-tech flows, deletion practices, and independent measurements.

### Government–technology coupling
**Evidence:** High for procurement/use; variable for particular data flows. GAO documents federal facial-recognition use and increasing AI acquisition.
**Next audit:** contracts, privacy impact assessments, inspector-general findings, procurement databases, litigation, statutory authorities, and audit logs.

### Behavioral manipulation
**Evidence:** Strong for targeted persuasion, ranking, engagement optimization, and design effects; insufficient for literal thought control.
**Next audit:** randomized studies, platform experiments, recommender audits, ad libraries, influence-operation records, and independent replication.

### Body as data source
**Evidence:** Strong in ordinary biometric/wearable contexts; extraordinary claims beyond measured sensors need separate proof.
**Next audit:** device permissions, biometric templates, health-data rules, broker sales, and government acquisition pathways.

### “Mental illness is fabricated”
**Evidence:** Not supported as a universal claim and inconsistent with current clinical/epidemiological evidence.
**Narrower questions worth auditing:** diagnostic expansion, pharmaceutical incentives, insurance reimbursement, coercive treatment history, workplace stress, conflicts of interest, and unequal access.

### Economic discipline
**Evidence:** Testable without assuming conspiracy.
**Measures:** wages versus productivity, housing affordability, household debt, healthcare costs, working time, labor bargaining power, wealth distribution, and social mobility.

## Failure modes
- Correlation is not coordination.
- Procurement is not proof of unrestricted access.
- Algorithmic influence is not proof of thought control.
- A flawed diagnosis or abusive institution does not establish that all mental illness is invented.
- A dramatic anecdote is a lead, not a conclusion.
- Absence of public evidence is not proof of either innocence or conspiracy.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Primary laws, contracts, datasets, court records, technical documentation.
2. GAO, inspectors general, regulators, legislative audits.
3. Peer-reviewed research and reproducible technical studies.
4. High-quality investigative reporting with documents.
5. Testimony, leaks, social posts, and anonymous claims requiring corroboration.

## Required claim record
For every future claim preserve: exact proposition; source URL; publication date; source type; evidence excerpt/paraphrase; confidence; counterevidence; alternative explanations; unresolved questions; and the observation that would change the conclusion.
